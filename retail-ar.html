PLATFORM + ECOSYSTEM STRATEGY

Retail AR Ecosystem Strategy

A competitive platform analysis of retail augmented reality, focusing on how Apple and Meta can attract producers, strengthen network effects and create more defensible ecosystem positions.

Two different platform plays

AppleARKit + Quick Look lower technical friction for retailers and developers while tying 3D commerce assets more closely to the iOS ecosystem.
MetaCreator tooling and social distribution let brands build AR experiences where discovery and commerce already happen.

Strategic mechanisms

Producer adoptionMake it easier for brands to create AR content
→
More experiencesIncrease the supply of useful retail AR
→
More consumer useStrengthen cross-side network effects
→
More investmentRaise ecosystem value and switching costs

Recommendations

Apple · lower 3D creation costUse AI/computer vision to make 3D product-asset creation accessible to smaller retailers.
Apple · improve matchingMove from “visualize the product I already chose” toward context-aware product recommendations.
Meta · strengthen standardsIncrease control over critical infrastructure to reduce frictionless multi-homing.
Meta · behavioral matchingUse social and commerce signals to improve product-user relevance and reinforce the data loop.

My contribution

Large-platform-owner analysis and recommendations, with emphasis on Apple and Meta, platform economics, producer incentives, switching costs and future strategic moves.
