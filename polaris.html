AI PRODUCT + PRODUCT ANALYTICS

Polaris — AI Career Copilot

An AI product concept for the USC MSBA community that connects job fit, skill-gap planning, alumni discovery and personalized outreach in one recruiting workflow.

From pain points to product requirements

“Which roles actually fit me?”→ Job Fit Engine
“What am I missing?”→ Tailored Action Plan
“Who should I contact?”→ Alumni / outreach mapping
“What should I say?”→ Personalized Email Copilot

End-to-end user journey

ResumeParse background and skills
→
AI profileStructure experience + goals
→
Job matchScore fit + expose gaps
→
Warm outreachFind alumni + draft message

How I thought about measurement

Success should be measured as a funnel, not a vanity metric
AdoptionActive users, repeat use
→
ActionFit checks, skill actions, warm intros
→
Career outcomesInterviews, time to offer, placement
→
EcosystemAlumni engagement, repeat employers

Implementation concept

The team proposed a small cohort pilot first, followed by broader Marshall rollout and deeper alumni integration. This reduces product risk while generating behavioral data to validate whether the matching and outreach features actually change recruiting outcomes.

My contribution

Business context / problem framing and the impact & success-metrics framework, alongside team ideation and presentation development.

Important: Polaris was a product concept and prototype. KPI values are pilot targets or proposed measures, not achieved production results.
